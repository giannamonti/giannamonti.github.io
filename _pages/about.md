I am an Associate Professor of Statistics (STAT-01/A) at the Department of Economics, Management, and Statistics (DEMS)](https://www.dems.unimib.it/en) at the [University of Milano-Bicocca](https://dems.unimib.it/en).

My research is deeply rooted in compositional data analysis, robust statistics, and nonparametric inference, with a strong emphasis on ecological applications. Currently, I am focused on the challenges of high-dimensional microbiome data, specifically developing sparse regression models designed to control the false discovery rate (FDR).

My methodological toolkit also includes extensive experience in mixture models and Bayesian hierarchical modeling.


I am a member of the [Italian Statistical Association (SIS)](https://www.sis-statistica.it/) and the [International Association for Statistical Computing (IASC-ISI)](https://iasc-isi.org/). I contribute to several research centers, including the [Health and Environmental Sustainability Research Center (POLARIS)](https://polaris.unimib.it/en/research/), [Bicocca Applied Statistics Center (B-ASC)](https://basc.dems.unimib.it/), [Milan Center for Neuroscience (NeuroMI)](https://neuromi.it/), and the [Bicocca Research Centre in Health Services (BReCHS)](https://en.unimib.it/ugov/organizationunit/362573). I have been elected Vice President of the [CODA Association](https://www.coda-association.org/en/) for the 2025–2029 term.

Since 2024, I have been an Associate Editor for the journal <i class="ai ai-springer ai-fw"></i> [Statistical Methods and Applications](https://link.springer.com/journal/10260), contributing to the peer review process and advancing statistical research.
