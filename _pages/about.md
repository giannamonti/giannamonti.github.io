I hold the position of Associate Professor of Statistics within the Department of Economics, Management, and Statistics at the [University of Milano-Bicocca](https://dems.unimib.it/en), Italy. My academic journey is profoundly anchored in the field of statistical analysis, with a primary focus on compositional data analysis, robust statistics, nonparametric inference, and statistical methods tailored for ecological data. My recent research endeavors prioritize high-dimensional compositional data and the analysis of microbiome data, specifically within the context of sparse regression models aimed at regulating the false discovery rate. Additionally, I have investigated mixture models and Bayesian hierarchical modeling in my prior work works.


Since 2024, I have been an Associate Editor for the journal [Statistical Methods and Applications](https://link.springer.com/journal/10260), contributing to the peer review process and advancing statistical research.
