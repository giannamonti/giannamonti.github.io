I hold the position of Associate Professor of Statistics (STAT-01/A) within the [Department of Economics, Management, and Statistics(DEMS)](https://www.dems.unimib.it/en) at the [University of Milano-Bicocca](https://dems.unimib.it/en), Italy. My academic journey is profoundly anchored in the field of statistical analysis, with a primary focus on compositional data analysis, robust statistics, nonparametric inference, and statistical methods tailored for ecological data. My recent research endeavors prioritize high-dimensional compositional data and the analysis of microbiome data, specifically within the context of sparse regression models aimed at controlling the false discovery rate. Furthermore, I have explored mixture models and Bayesian hierarchical modeling in my prior research.

I am a member of the [Italian Statistical Association(SIS)](https://www.sis-statistica.it/) and the [International Association for Statistical Computing (IASC-ISI)](https://iasc-isi.org/). I contribute to several research centers, including the [Health and Environmental Sustainability Research Center (POLARIS)](https://polaris.unimib.it/en/research/), [Bicocca Applied Statistics Center (B-ASC)](https://basc.dems.unimib.it/), [Milan Center for Neuroscience (NeuroMI)](https://neuromi.it/), and the [Bicocca Research Centre in Health Services (BReCHS)](https://en.unimib.it/ugov/organizationunit/362573). I have been elected Vice President of the [CODA Association](https://www.coda-association.org/en/) for the 2025–2029 term.

Since 2024, I have been an Associate Editor for the journal [Statistical Methods and Applications](https://link.springer.com/journal/10260), contributing to the peer review process and advancing statistical research.
